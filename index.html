Plan it. Live it. Relive it.

Farenwide is a travel app for planning your days, journaling as you go, and playing your trip back on the map — on your own or with your travel party.

Coming soon to iPhone and Android

Plan

Pin places, arrange them by day, and see the route between them by foot, car or train — even drag it onto the streets you prefer.

Journal

Record your path as you travel and add notes, photos, videos, voice memos and stickers right where they happened.

Playback

Watch your trip unfold on the map, day by day, or turn it into a vertical story video of your photos with the map alongside.

Travel party

Share a trip with friends: plan together, choose which moments to share, and see everyone’s journey in one playback.
