Support

Questions, ideas or something not working? Email support@projectAlamo.com.au and we’ll get back to you.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Solo trips work without one and stay on your phone. You only need an account to share a trip with a travel party.

How do I join a friend’s trip? Ask them for an invite code (Party tab → Invite), then tap Join a party on your trips list and enter it.

Who can see my journal? Only you, unless you open an item and switch on Share with travel party.

How do I delete my data? See Delete your data.
